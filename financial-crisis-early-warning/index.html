Still judgment calls (read, don't score)

  • Read the new Fed/IMF/BIS/ECB stability reports when flagged UPDATED above: look for private-credit leverage, hedge-fund basis-trade and AI-valuation language.
  • Hyperscaler earnings calls: did capex guidance rise, and did the stock fall anyway? Read the transcript, not just the price reaction.
  • Treasury auction TAIL (auction yield vs when-issued yield) is not in free data; bid-to-cover and indirect share are scored, but check press coverage of weak auctions.
  • AI-lab financing structure (circular deals, vendor financing, SPV terms): the news monitor surfaces headlines, the judgment of whether it is stress is yours.
  • Politics: shutdown / debt-limit dates (a shutdown blacks out BLS/BEA data), Fed chair and independence rulings, tariff court rulings - tracked via the news monitor, not scored.
  • Private-credit default rates (Fitch/KBRA/Proskauer) are paywalled or irregular: use the news-search results above.
